# Acknowledgements

## Data sources
Parish names, addresses, websites, and other basic facts in this directory come from the [Episcopal Asset Map](https://www.episcopalassetmap.org/), a map of Episcopal Church congregations, schools, and ministries of The Episcopal Church. Some details also come from parish and diocesan websites. Thank you to the parish and diocesan staff who keep those listings current.

Entries marked as **"Verified"** were confirmed through personal contact with or visits to parishes. See the [Terms and Conditions](https://episcopalcompass.org/terms.html) for more on how data from this site may be reused.

## Map
Map data © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), available under the Open Database License. Map tiles © [CARTO](https://carto.com/attributions). Place and ZIP code lookups are provided by [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org/), using OpenStreetMap data. The map is displayed with [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/), © Volodymyr Agafonkin and Leaflet contributors.

Diocesan boundaries are drawn from county outlines in the U.S. Census Bureau’s [cartographic boundary files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/carto-boundary-file.html) (2017 edition), via [us-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas) by Mike Bostock. The Diocese of Navajoland follows the Navajo Nation Reservation boundary from the Census Bureau’s [TIGERweb](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/). The assignment of counties to dioceses is this site’s own and is approximate; boundaries are not an official publication of the U.S. Census Bureau or The Episcopal Church.
