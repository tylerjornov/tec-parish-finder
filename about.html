About this list

This is a directory of Episcopal Church parishes in the United States and territories. The point is to make parish facts easy to filter. It is not an official publication of The Episcopal Church.

Information about women’s ordination and same-sex marriage are included for clarity. They are not here to campaign for or against any particular position on those practices. People who care about those questions — on either side — deserve to have the information before they walk into a parish. People who do not care can ignore the filters.

The person who created and owns this site is would personally identify as an Anglo-Catholic, and is a gay man who accepts the practice of women's ordination. His personal views are not reflected in the data, which is compiled from public sources.

Verification marks:

  • Verified — the site owner has personally visited the parish, or an official representative (e.g. a rector or vestry member) has confirmed the information.
  • Unverified — this information was compiled from public sources, and may contain errors.
  • Blank cells mean no available data

Distance sort uses as-the-crow-flies miles. A parish with no coordinates still appears in the list. The default is “closest in this result set,” regardless of how far away that may be.
