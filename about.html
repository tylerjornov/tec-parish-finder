About this list

This is an unofficial directory of Episcopal parishes in the United States, designed to make parishes easy to find and filter.

People deserve to know where a parish stands before they show up to a service. A broad-church progressive, a conservative evangelical, and an inclusive orthodox Anglo-Catholic should all be able to use this list and find it valuable.

What this site is

Parish websites often leave certain practical and doctrinal questions unanswered. Will this parish solemnize a same-sex marriage? Does it call women as rectors? Will Eucharistic prayers be said Ad Orientum or Versus Populum? Often, someone needs to engage in a long series of back-and-forth emails with the rector or spend years attending before finding out.

Episcopal Compass gathers those facts in one place. It does not grade a parish for where they stand on the issues, or for how plainly its own website states the information. It just serves to help people find the information they’re looking for.
