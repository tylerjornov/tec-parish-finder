About this list

This is a directory of Episcopal Church parishes in the United States and territories. The point is to make parish facts easy to filter. It is not an official publication of The Episcopal Church.

Information about churchmanship, women’s ordination, same-sex marriage, and similar matters is included for clarity. This website does not exist to campaign for or against any particular position on those issues. People deserve to know where a parish stands before they show up to a service. This website is intended to be equally helpful to, for example, a broad church progressive, a conservative evangelical, or an inclusive orthodox Anglo-Catholic.

Verification marks:

  • Verified — the site owner has personally visited the parish, or an official representative (e.g. the rector/vicar/priest-in-charge, or warden) has confirmed the information.
  • Unverified — this information was compiled from public sources, and may contain errors.
